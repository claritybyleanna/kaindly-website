About KAINDLY

Helping People Lead AI With Confidence.

KAINDLY supports the journey from motivated individual to enterprise transformation, without losing sight of the humans at the center of every decision.

The Founders

Two Leaders. One Better Way Forward.

KAINDLY was founded at the intersection of organizational change, emerging technology, and a shared conviction that capability should never come at the expense of care.

BS

Barbara Salami

Co-Founder

Barbara brings decades of experience navigating complex organizational change with a focus on inclusion and sustainable impact. She has spent her career working at the intersection of strategy, people, and systems, always with an eye toward who gets left behind when change moves too fast.

Her work centers on building real capability rather than surface-level adoption, so technology transformation is shaped by the people who need to participate meaningfully.

LBW

Leanna Baker Williams

Co-Founder

Leanna is a systems thinker who has spent years helping leaders make sense of emerging technology without losing sight of the human beings at the center of every decision. Her approach combines strategic clarity with deep care for the practical realities of learning and change.

She is known for finding what actually works and designing experiences that leave people capable, not confused.

Why KAINDLY

Intention Over Anxiety.

Clarity to know what matters. Capability to act on it.

AI is reshaping how organizations create value, but for many leaders the transformation feels more overwhelming than exciting. The technology moves faster than organizations can adapt. Hype drowns out substance. Pressure to act conflicts with the need to understand.

KAINDLY exists to make this change navigable: not by chasing every development, but by building the judgment to know what matters and the practical skills to move with confidence.

Our name reflects a conviction that leading through AI change requires not just competence, but kindness toward teams navigating uncertainty, toward ourselves as we learn, and in how we build technology that affects everyone.
