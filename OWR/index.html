Workshop flow

Build, test, and connect

Three connected activities turn accurate context into possibilities, a truthful introduction, and a purposeful next conversation.

  1. UnderstandHelp AI understand your experience and what you want to be different.
  2. ExploreExplore current, adjacent and less obvious possibilities.
  3. TestUse two real examples, a 30-second introduction and reciprocal feedback.
  4. ClosingRecognize your Impact Ladder, save your plan and choose a future cue.
