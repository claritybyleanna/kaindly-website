      How a connection works

      1. Clarify what you want to learn or explore.
      2. Introduce yourself and what you can offer.
      3. Find someone with a relevant perspective.
      4. Connect by mutual agreement.
      5. Choose a next conversation or shared session.

      Separate choices, always

      Workbook information is available to facilitators as you work. Publishing a profile to attendees and sharing your email or LinkedIn after a mutual match are separate decisions. Other attendees do not see your worksheet.
