Five Learning Pathways

Start With What You Need to Do.

These pathways are organized around outcomes, not difficulty. Most people draw from all five as their fluency grows.

01

Decide

Evaluate tools, weigh trade-offs, and make AI decisions you can explain and defend.

In practice: Lead a team through a vendor decision without waiting for hype to settle.

02

Communicate

Sharpen writing, presentations, and stakeholder messages without losing your voice.

In practice: Turn dense research into a clear, confident final presentation.

03

Operate

Turn one-off wins into a repeatable personal system inside your daily work.

In practice: Run a weekly reporting workflow in a fraction of the time.

04

Build

Move from user to maker by assembling assistants, automations, and practical prototypes.

In practice: Build a working proof of concept before hiring a development team.

05

Lead

Set useful norms, teach with confidence, and guide responsible AI adoption.

In practice: Help a team move from AI anxiety to shared, everyday fluency.
