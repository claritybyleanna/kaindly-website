Today’s session

Today’s agenda

Part 1 builds the foundation and your AI brain. Part 2 turns that foundation into a coaching chief of staff.

  1. 8:00 – 8:10

    You open.

  2. 8:10 – 8:35

    Foundations.

    What AI is and isn’t, where human judgment stays, and the guardrails for using it inside Bracco. Everyone gets their hands in Copilot during this block.

  3. 8:35 – 9:35

    Exercise one: giving your AI a brain.

    Each person builds their own operating system in Copilot so it understands how they think, how they make decisions and how they sound. This is the longest block, since everyone works through their own context. If the team has their DISC results handy, bring them.

  4. 9:35 – 9:45

    Short break.

  5. 9:45 – 10:30

    Exercise two: building a chief of staff that coaches.

    Each person builds one they can keep using, and puts it to work on a real field ride, leveraging some of the coaching prompts Whitney shared.

  6. 10:30 – 10:45

    Questions and close.
